About Nishka

Bridging Business Utility & Homemaker Potential

Nishka was founded to create an organized, dignified work coordination system that connects growing businesses with dedicated homemakers in local communities.

By transforming unstructured manual work into well-coordinated tasks with clear instructions, material logistics, and quality assurance, Nishka ensures that merchants get reliable execution while homemakers gain meaningful earning opportunities.

Terms & conditions

Operating policy & terms summary

Nishka operates as a managed work coordination platform and does not position itself as a job agency or direct employer. Task allocations depend on active business requirements, suitability and availability. Homemaker participation is voluntary and task-based. Work, assignments or income are not guaranteed. Task scope, quality expectations, logistics and payment terms should be agreed before work begins.

Homemakers should review task details and payment terms before accepting an assignment. Businesses should provide accurate requirements and confirm scope, timing, handling instructions and commercial terms with Nishka.
