About Nishka

Building Opportunities. Supporting Businesses. Strengthening Families.

Our story

Creating a trusted bridge.

Our goal is to create a trusted bridge between businesses that need reliable support and homemakers who want meaningful opportunities to use their skills and time.

Mission

Meaningful opportunities for homemakers.

To create meaningful earning opportunities for homemakers while helping businesses grow through reliable and flexible workforce support.

Vision

A trusted pathway to suitable opportunities.

To build a clear, respectful process that helps people across communities explore suitable opportunities.

Privacy & Terms

Your trust and privacy are important to Nishka. This section explains how we handle information submitted through our website and the basic terms for using our platform.

Privacy Policy

Nishka may collect information that you voluntarily submit, including your name, phone number, email address, location, skills, availability and business requirements.

We use this information only to review registrations and enquiries, communicate with you, coordinate suitable opportunities and improve our services. Nishka does not sell your personal information.

You may request access, correction or deletion of your information by contacting us at info2nishka@gmail.com.

Terms & Conditions

Nishka connects businesses with homemakers and coordinates suitable work opportunities. Availability of work depends on business requirements, location, suitability and capacity.

Work details, timelines, quality requirements and payment terms will be communicated and agreed upon separately before work begins.

Users must provide accurate information, follow agreed instructions and use the platform responsibly. Please do not submit passwords, banking PINs or other highly sensitive information through website forms.
