About Nishka

Building Opportunities. Supporting Businesses. Strengthening Families.

Nishka is a brand of Avanza Solutions Private Limited created to connect businesses with homemakers for suitable work opportunities.

Our story

Creating a trusted bridge.

Our goal is to create a trusted bridge between businesses that need reliable support and homemakers who want meaningful opportunities to use their skills and time.

Mission

Meaningful opportunities for homemakers.

To create meaningful earning opportunities for homemakers while helping businesses grow through reliable and flexible workforce support.

Vision

A trusted source of income and opportunity.

To become a trusted source of income and opportunity for people across communities.
