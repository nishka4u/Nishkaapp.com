Help & clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Find accurate answers about our work coordination services for both businesses and homemakers.

▦   For Businesses

What type of work does Nishka coordinate?

Nishka coordinates repetitive, non-technical manual tasks such as product packaging, label/sticker application, SKU sorting, garment folding, promotional kit assembly, and gift hamper preparation.

How can a business submit a work requirement?

You can submit your task details, estimated quantities, and target timeline using the 'Submit a Work Requirement' form on this page or email us directly at info@nishkaapp.com.

How are task rates and quantities assessed?

Nishka reviews the task scope, material handling, quantity, quality requirements and timeline before sharing a quotation.

How does material handover work?

Materials can be picked up from your warehouse or delivered directly to our designated local hub in Bengaluru according to agreed scheduling terms.

How is completed work quality-checked?

Completed batches are checked against the agreed instructions and quality checklist before return and settlement.

♟   For Homemakers

Who can register with Nishka?

Homemakers in Bengaluru who are interested in suitable task-based work can submit their details for review.

Are there any registration or subscription fees?

No. Nishka does not charge mandatory registration or subscription fees.

Is task work guaranteed every day?

No. Task availability depends on active business requirements, location and suitability. Registration does not guarantee work or income.

How are task rates and payments processed?

The task rate and payment terms are explained before acceptance. Payments depend on completed and verified work according to agreed terms.
