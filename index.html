HELP & CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Find accurate answers about our work coordination services for both businesses and
homemakers.

▦ For Businesses

What type of work does Nishka coordinate?

Nishka coordinates suitable repetitive, non-technical tasks such as product packaging, label and sticker application, sorting and counting, garment or paper folding, gift kit assembly, and printed-material sorting, subject to business requirements.

How can a business submit a work requirement?

Use the “Submit a Work Requirement” form on the For Businesses page. Share your company and contact details, work category, approximate batch quantity, target completion date, handover location, and any special instructions. The Nishka team can then review the scope and contact you.

How are task rates and quantities assessed?

Rates and batch quantities are assessed based on the type and complexity of the task, estimated time, materials, volume, quality requirements, and agreed timelines. Pricing and per-unit terms are discussed before work is assigned.

How does material handover work?

Materials are handed over through an agreed coordination point or arrangement in Bengaluru. The handover plan, quantities, instructions, and return process are confirmed with the business before the batch begins.

How is completed work quality-checked?

Completed batches are checked against the agreed instructions and quality criteria. Any issues are reviewed with the relevant coordinator before the batch is confirmed for return and settlement.

♟ For Homemakers

Who can register with Nishka?

Homemakers in Bengaluru who are interested in suitable non-technical, task-based work can submit their details. Opportunities depend on location, availability, task requirements, and onboarding suitability.

Are there any registration or subscription fees?

No. Registration is free. Nishka does not charge mandatory registration or upfront subscription fees to join the platform.

Is task work guaranteed every day?

No. Task availability depends on active business requirements, location, batch volume, and suitability. Registering with Nishka does not guarantee daily tasks or a fixed income.

How are task rates and payments processed?

Task rates and payment terms are shared before a task is accepted. Payments are based on completed work that meets the agreed verification and quality requirements, according to the settlement terms communicated for that task.
