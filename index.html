FAQs

Questions, answered clearly.

Does joining Nishka require a registration fee?

No mandatory joining or registration fee is charged to homemakers.

Is work or income guaranteed?

No. Opportunities depend on active business requirements, suitability and availability. Income is not guaranteed.

What work can businesses submit?

Examples include suitable packing, labeling, sorting, folding, gift hamper preparation and kit assembly.

Where does Nishka operate?

Nishka is currently focused on Bengaluru, with opportunities depending on operational coverage and active requirements.
